## About / Overview (2,000 characters max)

nDimensions.Ai is a non-profit group of independent AI researchers. We build, test and publish AI work in the open, as a hobby and purely out of love for the field.

Our name comes from the space where AI lives: data and models exist in n dimensions. Our mark, ∂ⁿ, is the partial derivative raised to the power n, a nod to how every model learns, one partial derivative at a time.

What we work on
• PANINI CLI: an open-source AI tool for the command line, now on npm
• PINGALA Agent: an AI agent for thoughtful candidate screening (a research project, not a product)
• PANINI: a personal autonomous agent that works on your behalf
• PANINI-ANIMGX: a local animation studio driven by AI
• Decoding Language Models: an 84-episode series that takes language models apart, one idea at a time

We are not a company and we do not sell anything. Follow the page for research notes, open code and new episodes.

## Post 2 — Poster (image: ndimensions-poster-1080x1350.png)

θ ← θ − η · ∂L/∂θ

That one line is how almost every neural network learns: measure how the loss changes with each parameter, then take a small step downhill. Backpropagation is partial derivatives at scale.

It is also why our logo is ∂ⁿ. Every model learns one partial derivative at a time. We study all n of them.

## Post 8 — Research note template (image: ndimensions-post-research-note-1200x1200.png)

Research note 001: attention is a weighted average.

Each token looks at every other token and blends them, weighted by how relevant they are. Softmax keeps the weights positive and makes them sum to one.

attention(Q, K, V) = softmax(Q·Kᵀ / √d) · V
